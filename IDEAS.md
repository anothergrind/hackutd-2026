Ideas

**Maintenance Ticket Escalation Predictor**
Predict which maintenance requests will become major incidents.

- Classify tickets by urgency and risk.
- Connect related tickets by building, floor, equipment, and time.
- Nemotron turns technician notes into structured data and recommends priority.
- Demo: show several small complaints combining into one larger issue

**Energy Waste Investigator**
Detect abnormal energy consumption before it becomes expensive.

- Compare energy usage across similar buildings or zones.
- Identify inefficient HVAC, lighting, or equipment.
- Nemotron analyzes utility data and maintenance notes to identify likely causes.
- Demo: inject an abnormal energy pattern and generate an action plan.

A **commercial property risk and disaster-response platform** that:

- Predicts building issues before they escalate.
- Uses Nemotron to reason over sensor data, maintenance reports, weather, and property information.
- Simulates how a catastrophe affects a building or property portfolio.
- Produces prioritized response plans for facility managers and emergency teams

All of these ideas are meant to answer the NVIDIA and CBRE tracks in HackUTD
